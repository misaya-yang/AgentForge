  with 16 people listed on its payroll throughout every relevant workweek.
- `F-PARTTIME-FRIDAY`: Two of the 16 were hourly part-time employees who
  normally neither worked nor received compensation on Fridays, but neither
  employment relationship ended.
- `F-WEEKS-22`: That pattern existed for 22 calendar weeks in both the current
  and preceding calendar years.
- `F-RIVERA-PROTECTED-COMPLAINT`: On 2024-04-22, Rivera told HR that her

  believed the practice was sex discrimination.

  transfer was final; it became effective on 2024-05-13.
- `F-RIVERA-TRANSFER`: Rivera retained pay and title, but moved from a
  Monday-Friday hybrid schedule to rotating nights and weekends, lost

- `F-MALE-REPLACEMENT`: A less experienced male employee replaced Rivera in

- `F-MOTIVE-SEX`: A preserved manager message says the client-facing lead
  should be a man. Authentication and admissibility have not been litigated.
- `F-MOTIVE-COMPLAINT`: A preserved HR note attributes the transfer to

  and admissibility have not been litigated.
- `F-FILING-295`: On 2025-02-20, 295 days after final notice, Rivera jointly

## Pinned official legal sources

### `SRC-STATUTE-2000E`

- Authority: Government Publishing Office, United States Code, 2023 Edition.
- Status at cutoff: controlling statutory text.
- Locator: 42 U.S.C. § 2000e(b), current through 2024-01-03.
- Official URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap21-subchapVI-sec2000e.htm
- Fixed excerpt: Employer means a person affecting commerce with 15 or more
  employees for each working day in each of 20 or more calendar weeks in the
  current or preceding calendar year, subject to the statutory exclusions.

### `SRC-STATUTE-2000E-2`

- Authority: Government Publishing Office, United States Code, 2023 Edition.
- Status at cutoff: controlling statutory text.
- Locator: 42 U.S.C. § 2000e-2(a)(1), current through 2024-01-03.
- Official URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap21-subchapVI-sec2000e-2.htm
- Fixed excerpt: An employer may not discriminate with respect to
  compensation, terms, conditions, or privileges of employment because of sex
  or another enumerated trait.

### `SRC-STATUTE-2000E-3`

- Authority: Government Publishing Office, United States Code, 2023 Edition.
- Status at cutoff: controlling statutory text.
- Locator: 42 U.S.C. § 2000e-3(a), current through 2024-01-03.
- Official URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap21-subchapVI-sec2000e-3.htm
- Fixed excerpt: An employer may not discriminate because an employee opposed
  a practice made unlawful by Title VII or participated in a Title VII
  investigation, proceeding, or hearing.

### `SRC-STATUTE-2000E-5`

- Authority: Government Publishing Office, United States Code, 2023 Edition.
- Status at cutoff: controlling statutory text.
- Locator: 42 U.S.C. § 2000e-5(e)(1), current through 2024-01-03.
- Official URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap21-subchapVI-sec2000e-5.htm
- Fixed excerpt: The ordinary charge deadline is 180 days. If proceedings are
  initially instituted with an authorized state or local agency, the period is
  300 days after the practice or 30 days after notice that the agency
  terminated proceedings, whichever is earlier.

### `SRC-SCOTUS-WALTERS-1997`

- Authority: Supreme Court of the United States.
- Status at cutoff: controlling precedent.
- Locator: *Walters v. Metropolitan Educational Enterprises, Inc.*, 519 U.S.
  202, 207-212 (1997).
- Official URL: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/519bv.pdf
- Fixed excerpt: The payroll method asks whether an employment relationship
  existed, rather than only whether a person worked or received compensation
  on the day. The touchstone is employment relationships with 15 or more
  people for each working day in 20 or more weeks.

### `SRC-CA8-MULDROW-2022`

- Authority: United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit.
- Status at cutoff: judgment vacated by the Supreme Court; included as the
  conflicting historical source.
- Locator: *Muldrow v. City of St. Louis*, 30 F.4th 680, 688-689 (8th Cir.
  2022).
- Official URL: https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/22/04/202975P.pdf
- Fixed excerpt: The court used a materially-significant-disadvantage standard
  and rejected minor changes without that disadvantage.

### `SRC-SCOTUS-MULDROW-2024`

- Authority: Supreme Court of the United States.
- Status at cutoff: controlling precedent.
- Locator: *Muldrow v. City of St. Louis*, No. 22-193, slip op. 5-11 (Apr. 17,
  2024).
- Official URL: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-193_q86b.pdf
- Fixed excerpt: A transfer-discrimination plaintiff must show some injury
  respecting employment terms or conditions, but no significant-harm test.
  The Court vacated the Eighth Circuit judgment, distinguished Burlington's
  retaliation standard, and left proof and forfeiture questions for remand.

### `SRC-SCOTUS-BURLINGTON-2006`

- Authority: Supreme Court of the United States.
- Status at cutoff: controlling precedent.
- Locator: *Burlington Northern & Santa Fe Railway Co. v. White*, 548 U.S. 53,
  61-73 (2006).
- Official URL: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/548bv.pdf
- Fixed excerpt: Retaliation is not confined to workplace or employment-term
  actions, but requires material adversity that might dissuade a reasonable
  worker from making or supporting a discrimination charge; context matters.

### `SRC-EEOC-FILING-CHARGE`

- Authority: United States Equal Employment Opportunity Commission.
- Status at cutoff: official procedural guidance.
- Locator: Filing a Charge, paragraphs 17-19.
- Official URL: https://www.eeoc.gov/fact-sheet/filing-charge
- Fixed excerpt: Title VII generally uses 180 days; in a jurisdiction with an
  authorized antidiscrimination agency, an EEOC charge may be filed within 300
  days, or 30 days after notice of state or local termination, whichever is
  earlier.
