# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want AI Gateway to be a respectful, practical, and technically rigorous project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate constructively, focus on the work, and help keep the project welcoming for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Keep review feedback about code, behavior, tests, security, and maintainability.
- Respect privacy and do not expose secrets, personal data, or private operational details.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, release risk, and safety gates.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately bypassing security, validation, or release-safety requirements.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests after maintainers have set scope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; restrict participation; or escalate to GitHub moderation when needed. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on keeping the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

For conduct issues, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile. For vulnerabilities or abuse paths, follow `SECURITY.md`.
